# Privacy Policy - Stips Download

Last updated: September 29, 2026

**Stips Download** ("the Extension") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled.

## 1. Zero External Data Collection
- The Extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell** any of your personal data, messages, chat history, or credentials to any external server, third party, or analytics service.
- The Extension has no remote backend or tracking telemetry.

## 2. Direct Communication with Stips
- All network requests made by the Extension are directed strictly and solely to `stips.co.il` endpoints using your current, active browser session cookies and credentials (`credentials: "include"`).
- These requests are performed solely to fetch your personal chat history upon your explicit request.

## 3. Local Storage & Security
- Downloaded chat data, resumption checkpoints, and preferences are stored exclusively on your local computer using the browser's built-in `IndexedDB` and `chrome.storage.local`.
- Exported archives (HTML, JSON, TXT) are generated on-the-fly entirely within your browser and saved directly to your device's Downloads directory.
- You may purge all locally cached data at any time via the extension popup or by clearing browser site data.
